## Custom Domain & Business Email Note
To receive external emails at `compliance@unifiedmulticonceptsltd.com` / `info@unifiedmulticonceptsltd.com`, register `unifiedmulticonceptsltd.com` with a domain registrar, configure MX/SPF/DKIM/DMARC records with Google Workspace or Zoho Mail, and link the domain in **Settings → Pages → Custom domain**.
